Snapshot view, not history. USAJobs reports each posting's current status — there is no event log of when status changed. Many agencies (DOJ, DHS, Navy, etc.) never report “Candidate selected”; their postings go straight from “Applications under review” to “Job closed.” A 0% selected rate usually reflects that reporting practice, not slow hiring.

“Median posting days” below is days the announcement was open (closeDate − openDate), not time-to-hire.

Short application windows

Postings whose announcement was open for 5 days or fewer (closeDate − openDate ≤ 5). Short windows often indicate that an internal candidate is already lined up, or the hire is targeted at a specific person/group. Restricted to postings with a non-null close date. When no filter is applied, agencies/series need ≥50 postings to appear; with a filter active, that floor is dropped to 1.
